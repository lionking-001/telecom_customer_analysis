# NINJA TELECOMS – Customer Profiling & Segmentation Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on customer profiling and segmentation for **Ninja Telecoms** using predefined scoring criteria supplied by the business. Customers were evaluated based on their **data purchase consistency (Loyalty)** and **bonus data usage behavior** over a six-month period.

Using computed scores, customers were classified into **four priority tiers** to support targeted service delivery, incentives, and marketing strategies.

---

## Problem Statement
Ninja Telecoms seeks to perform comprehensive customer profiling to better understand customer behavior and improve decision-making around loyalty programs, incentives, and service prioritization.

However, raw subscription and bonus usage data alone do not clearly indicate:
- Which customers are truly loyal  
- Which customers rely heavily on bonus incentives  
- How customers should be objectively grouped for targeted engagement  

The task was to apply predefined scoring rules to evaluate customer behavior, compute total scores, and classify customers into actionable tiers.

---

## Objectives of the Analysis
- Analyze total customers, subscriptions, and data usage  
- Apply predefined scoring rules to compute:
  - Loyalty Score  
  - Bonus Usage Score  
- Generate a total customer score  
- Classify customers into **Tier 1 – Tier 4** based on percentage thresholds  
- Analyze customer distribution, usage, and performance across tiers and cities  
- Provide data-driven recommendations based on observed behavior

## Methodology
- Applied predefined scoring rules to compute loyalty and bonus usage scores  
- Combined scores to generate total customer scores  
- Segmented customers into **Tier 1 – Tier 4** based on score thresholds  
- Analyzed performance by city, tier, and month  

---

## Key Insights
- Loyalty and bonus usage are related but not perfectly correlated  
- Tier 3 customers show the most balanced engagement and upsell potential  
- Bonus incentives show diminishing returns at higher loyalty tiers  
- Customer engagement is strongest in **Abuja** and **Lagos**  
- Retail customers make up **99%** of all customers  

---

## Recommendations
- Reduce cost-heavy bonus data for Tier 1 customers; offer perks such as priority support, faster peak-hour speeds, and exclusive plan upgrades  
- Target Tier 3 customers with milestone-based offers to encourage upgrades and maximize ROI on incentives  
- Send SMS or in-app notifications showing balances, expiry dates, and usage tips to boost bonus utilization  
- Run small experiments with discounted or comeback bundles and scale spending only after positive behavior changes  
- Replicate high-performing campaigns from top cities while customizing promotions and infrastructure upgrades for underperforming locations
